Trace scoring — calibration

Not shipped: this page exists so the numbers in WRITE are set from a measurement rather than from taste. It reads them out of js/write.js and calls the real wScore(), so it calibrates the scorer the app runs, not a copy of it. Eight synthetic traces are put over all 24 letters. middle, jitter 5 and tail should pass; oversize, scribble and line should not; the two heavier jitters are the boundary and may go either way. middle is the row the metric exists for: it traces the letter's own centreline, so it should read at or near 100 for every letter. The area scorer this replaced gave it 70 on psi and 88 on alpha — the same flawless trace, marked differently because one glyph is thicker than the other. It is sampled at a different step from the scorer on purpose, so that 100 is measured rather than assumed; see the note above skeleton(). The guide is drawn in whatever serif the machine resolves, so absolute numbers shift a little between devices; the gap between the groups is what matters.
