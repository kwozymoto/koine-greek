How this app works

Lessons

Twenty-seven chapters, from the letters to reading the text, written to teach on their own. Each comes in short parts with questions along the way, and a video lecture if you want one. They follow the order of a standard first-year grammar, David Alan Black's Learn to Read New Testament Greek, so they line up if you are using it.

Drill

Pick what to practise.

Read

Tap any word for its meaning, and for its parsing — what form the word is in.

Look up

Any word in the course, and every paradigm — the tables of endings. One box: type agape or love for a word, aorist or genitive for a table.
